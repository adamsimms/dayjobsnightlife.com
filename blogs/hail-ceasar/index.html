Hail Ceasar!

March 20, 2016
Blair Dohey
Blogs, Loves

Fans of L’Gros Luxe know that they make one hell of a bloody Caesar! With ingredients like Burgers, Quesidellas, Grilled Cheese, Fried Pickles, Onion Rings and more, the tasty cocktail is more like a meal than a cocktail. 

During the month of March, L’Gros Luxe in collaboration with Motts Clamato and Jim Beam are running a cool contest that encourages you to build the craziest Caesar you can imagine. Once you’ve piled up the ingredients, post a picture of it to Instagram featuring the hashtag, #LGROSLUXECAESAR

Contestants will have a chance to win one of 5 Clamato snowboards, gift certificates and bottles of Jim Beam and of course you get to drink/eat your creation after you’ve snapped the perfect photo. All the info you will need can be found in these photos.

Don’t forget to download the L’Gros Luxe app to be sure to get a seat in your favourite L’Gros Luxe location. Details on where to download can be found here!
