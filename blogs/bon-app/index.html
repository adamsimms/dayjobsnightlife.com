Bon App

October 11, 2015
Blair Dohey
Blogs, Loves

In one year, L’Gros Luxe has quickly expanded into a homegrown chain of restaurants offering delicious food at more than reasonable prices. With 3 locations in Montreal, one on the South Shore, a new location opening in Quebec City and more on the way it’s easier than ever to drop in for a great meal and a couple of drinks.

Being as popular as L’Gros Luxe has been, people continue to line-up to wait for a coveted table but that’s about to change. For the last few months the folks behind the eclectically decorated restaurants have been developing an App to help reduce the wait times and shorten those lines outside their restaurants. The App couldn’t come at a more perfect time as Mother Nature has once again started giving us the cold shoulder.

The App is beautiful while the concept is simple. You open the App and either navigate around the cool features or you chose the restaurant you wish to eat at. Leave your contact information and the number of people in your party and be sure to be near the restaurant (at least 15 minutes away) and when your number is up they will contact you directly and tell you to come on in. If you chose to change your mind, you simply cancel your request.

You can download the app here for iPhone and here for Android.
