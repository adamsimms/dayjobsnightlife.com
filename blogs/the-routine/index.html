The Routine

March 13, 2017
Blair Dohey
Blogs, Blurr

Guys, whether you’re pounding away the hours at your 9 to 5 or you’re making your own hours and your own rules, we all must do our best to stay in shape and look good doing it. Jack Black has been on our radar ever since we had a friend smuggle some of that fantastic Turbo Wash shampoo into Canada a couple of years ago (It was a life changer).  If you’re hitting the gym before work or before a hot date, here’s four of our favorite Jack Black products that you need to have in your bag at all times!

1. Eye Balm De-puffing & Cooling Gel – A brand new product featuring a blend of hydrating ingredients, soothing botanical extracts, and nourishing vitamins help reduce the look of puffiness, soften the appearance of fine lines, and brighten tired-looking eyes. From the second those little metal roller balls hit your skin its jolted awake. $29

2. Clean Break – Oil-free Moisturizer – A lightweight burst of moisture leaves skin refreshed and healthier-looking with a natural, shine-free finish. Organic White Tea and Sea Kelp deliver antioxidant protection, while Niacinamide helps improve skin tone and texture.This is great for the guy who doesn’t want to spend to much time picking out a moisturizer. This works great, period. $39

3. Cool CTRL Natural Deodorant – This aluminum-free formula contains clinically tested, time-released odour-blocking technology plus natural anti-bacterial and anti-fungal ingredients to stop even the strongest odour. Featuring the popular Turbo Wash® aroma (remember that shampoo??), this natural deodorant provides a long-lasting, refreshed feeling, leaving you feeling confident that you don’t need strong perfumes to smell good. I will never go back to another brand. $22

4. Beard Oil – Enhance your beard’s appearance (if you have one) and the condition of the skin underneath. An exclusive blend of certified organic natural oils, potent antioxidants and vitamins make your beard soft to the touch and the person you’ll be kissing will thank you for it. Kalahari Melon Oil and Marula Oil offer a fast-absorbing hydration. Your skin is smoothed and your facial hair are softened. Brown Algae, and Carrot Extract are known for their anti-bacterial and anti-inflammatory properties. Vitamin E helps repair sun-damaged skin. Plum Oil softens brittle and dries facial hair for added control and healthy shine. All that to say that you’ll leave the gym looking better than when you went in. $28
