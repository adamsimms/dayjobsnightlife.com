Halloween Party Scene

October 22, 2016
Blair Dohey
Blogs, Loves

Halloween is an excellent time of the year to leave your old self behind for a night and dress up into something entirely new. Whether you choose to be sexy or scary, there’ll be no shortage of parties in Montreal where you can strut your stuff. From Studio 54 inspired disco at the Richmond to heading down the rabbit hole at Soubois, here’s a look 5 very different parties you can’t miss this Halloween! 

Yelloween at L’Auberge

The dead are celebrated at this year’s Yelloween at L’Auberge St-Gabriel. One of the biggest annual Halloween parties in Montreal, this year’s theme promises to be anything but stiff.

When: October 29 (Cocktail dinatoire starts at 7:00 – $68 taxes and service included; party starts at 10:00 – $30 pre-sale, $40 regular, $50 last minute)

Where: 426 Rue Saint Gabriel

Details: Music: DAF, Donald Lauture, Cabana, Adrien Mitchell, Carolyn Boisvert, Yrko

Tickets: Click for link

Dress code: Funeral chic

Monde Osé Halloween Ball

The St. James Theatre in Old Montreal will be doused in vibrant colour for Monde Osé’s outrageous Pop Halloween Ball. Gathering inspiration from pop culture, out-there icons, sex symbols, pop music, art and fashion, this is the party for those would really want to sex it up and step outside their comfort zones.

When: October 29th (tickets available until October 28th – $50)

Where: 265 Rue Saint-Jacques

Details: Pop art installations, live art (nude bodypainting, burlesques performances, tantalizing video projections)

Tickets: Click for link

Dress code: Dress in pop colours, latex. lingerie and make-up as your favourite pop music star or sex symbol.

Down The Rabbit Hole – Soubois

The enchanted forest of Soubois will be entirely transformed and while you are hosted by Soubois’ take on iconic Disney characters. An intriguing premise brought to life by one of the city’s most happening gathering places.

When: October 28th and 29th

Where: 1106 Maisonneuve Blvd W

Details: DJ’s Thomas H, Pat Boogie, Leyla, Ann Forest, Greenwood

RSVP: rsvp@soubois.com; 514-564-3672

Dress code: Come inspired

Halloween Disco Party 54 – Le Richmond

Studio 54 is still to this day the most infamous nightclub in North America. Nothing has come close to this nightlife legend. Cher was there and Andy Warhol and his wig were also regular features. The Richmond is known throughout Montreal for pull out all the stops when it comes to their parties and their Studio 54 inspired, Disco bonanza will no doubt be as crazy as everything else they pull off.

When: October 29th, 9:00pm ($50)

Where: 377 Rue Richmond

Details: VIP Booths available, canapés will be served, 

RSVP and Information:  ibti@lerichmond.com; (514) 508-8749

Dress code: Studio 54

Atlantis – Buonanotte 

An Atlantis inspired Halloween party at Buonanotte will no doubt attract a sea of people to the Main. Because Buona doesn’t do subtle, this party is expected to be as wild as ever. An Atlantis theme a Buonanotte is also something I’m you’ll have to see to believe.

When: October 28th and 29th

Where: 3518 Boul St-Laurent

RSVP: 514 848 0644

Details: DJ YO-C, Fafa Khan, Manny Occean

Dress code: Atlantis
