Merry Christmas!

December 23, 2014
Blair Dohey
Blogs, Blurr

2014 has been a big year for us here at Day Jobs and the Nightlife and we are happy to share our success with you. From St. John’s, Newfoundland to Victoria, B.C., and around the globe you keep coming back for more and we couldn’t be more grateful. As the year draws to a close we want to thank for continuing to follow us as we zig-zag through the urban jungle to bring you the best as you’ve come to expect it.

This year has seen no shortage of crazy ups and downs for our fair city. The focus currently spotlighting Griffintown’s continued growth and the supposed darkening former bright spots like Globe on St. Laurent, reminds us all of the oscillations a city must face as it grows and changes. Whether it’s seen as good or bad, we all must agree that there’s never a dull moment in the 514. Day Jobs and the Nightlife is out there, in the thick of all the highs our city has to offer as well as the challenges we face, bringing our points of view to you.

We love Montreal and we hope that 2015 is a successful year for everyone on all fronts. Have a wonderful holiday season and we are looking forward to delivering even more interesting reads and dazzling photography in the New Year.

Peace, joy, health and happiness to one and all.

Blair
