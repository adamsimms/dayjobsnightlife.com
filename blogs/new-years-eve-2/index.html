New Year’s Eve

December 10, 2016
Blair Dohey
Blogs, Loves

There isn’t another night of the year in Montreal, or the world, that has more pressure on its shoulders to be the biggest night of the year. On New Year’s Eve in the city, hundreds of thousands of people will be out on the town, dressed to impress and looking for that special kiss when the clock strikes midnight. Luckily Montreal is more than prepared to deliver an excellent New Year’s Eve with these not-to-be-missed-parties! 

Auberge Saint-Gabriel

La second avant minuit!

Details:

7:00pm Gastronomic Dinner (check out the full menu here, it looks good!),  $165.00 per person.

10:00pm Soirée only; $40 per person.

Music: Christian Pronovost, DAF, Cabana and Adrien Mitchell

Reservations: réveillon@aubergesaintgabriel.com; 438-985-2156

W Montreal

New Year No Rules

Details:

Wunderbar & Plateau Lounge: 9:00pm-3:00am; No cover charge

Music: Jojo Flores, Toddy Flores, Quest, Fadel, Donald Lauture

Reservations: info@thebpcworld.com; 514-395-3195

É.A.T.: L’Ultime – All you can eat wet bar, cote de boeuf, stripped bass, dessert table + access to your choice of Wunderbar or Plateau Lounge, late-night snack table and access to detox/retox brunch; $1000.00 per couple.

Reservations: info@thebpcworld.com; 514-395-3195

La Société

Nouvel An Titi Parisien

Details:

5:00pm – Welcome 5a7 cocktail (it would be best to arrive between 5 and 7:00pm); 6 course meal $95 per person + $40 wine pairing per person

Menu: see above

Reservations: montreal.lasociete.com/reservations; 514-507-9223

Au Sommet

New Year’s Eve Celebration at Les Enfants Terribles

Details:

At Les Enfants Terribles: From 8:00pm – 4 course meal $75 per person – excellent view of the city!!

Au Sommet: Enjoy surprises, champagne and chocolate from 10:00pm white counting down to the 2017 from high atop the 360 degree observation deck on the roof of Place Ville Marie. A DJ will keep you moving while the awesome view of the city will take your breath away. Tickets are $40 and going fast.

Music: A DJ will be on hand to set the soundtrack for the evening

Reservations: 514-544-8884

SOMWHR

Details:

From 9:00pm and starting at $75 per person (presale – $100 at the door before midnight – $30 after midnight), open bar from 9:00pm to midnight; Sit down tables, $1500 (for 10 tickets); Stand up tables, $1000 (for 8 tickets).

Padore

Midnight at the Penthouse

Details:

From 9:00pm to 11:00pm, dinner, open bar and champagne tower; $90-120 per person. Bottle service starting at 11pm; Evening tickets $40 per person.
