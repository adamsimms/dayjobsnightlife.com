Terrasse sur l’Auberge

June 15, 2014
Blair Dohey
Blogs, Loves

It’s a great thing to look out over your balcony and see the world beyond. At the terrasse sur l’Auberge, located on the rooftop of the hotel L’Auberge du Vieux Port, they take a panoramic view to the next level.

Coinciding with the Grand Prix, l’Auberge and Notable threw a stylish rooftop party for the annual inauguration of their terrasse. Serving their VIP guests Moët et Chandon and canapés well into the evening, we were also treated to the lofty tunes of DJ Eleven.

The Terrasse sur l’Auberge is one of the most amazing views in the city and every time I go back it’s like seeing the awe inducing view for the first time. From any vantage point you can see the vast St. Laurence river flow by and dotted by the majestic building in old Montreal. Until the end of June we also have a colourful view of the Cirque du Soleil’s yellow and blue big top. As I wrote in one of my first articles, the terrasse is also a great place to catch our fireworks festival.

I’ve been coming here for years and the things that keep me coming back are simple. The beautiful view, the great service and proximity to all Montreal has to offer. The bar for Style and substance has never been higher.
