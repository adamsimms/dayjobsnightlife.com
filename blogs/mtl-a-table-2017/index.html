MTL à Table 2017

October 21, 2017
Blair Dohey
Blogs, Loves

It’s back! The 2017 edition of MTL à Table is just around the corner and this year promised to be bigger than ever. Coinciding with the Montreal’s 375th anniversary celebrations, this year’s MTL a Table extends a full two weeks will feature 175 restaurants located all over the city.

Each of the 175 participating restaurants (see full list here) feature a set menu at either $21, $31 or $41 and some of these restaurants will also feature a brunch menu set at $15! It’s a great way to discover something new or rediscover an old favourite.

All restaurants will feature a menu created especially for the event and each menu will feature ingredients sourced right here in Quebec. Montreal and all our surrounding boroughs feature a wealth of diverse products that something special to any meal, whether we are eating out or dining at home.

In addition to food being prepared in the restaurants, MTL a Table’s 2017 edition also features Exclusive Aeroplan Privileges, Wine events presented by the SAQ, Chocolatey desserts by Cacao Barry, Guided Neighbourhood tours, Special restaurant events and more.

MTL a Table will run from November 2nd to the 16th and reservations at your favourite restaurants are highly recommended. This is one of the most anticipated food events of the year and hungry foodies have already begun clamouring for the chance to try something new.

For more information, check out the MTL a Table website and be sure to follow us here for more.
