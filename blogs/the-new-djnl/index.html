The New DJNL

November 27, 2016
Blair Dohey
Blogs, Loves

We are very excited and proud to present the brand spanking new Day Jobs Nightlife! We’ve spend much of 2016 developing this exciting and sparkly new look while adding a few great new features that will improve your experience on our site. Since 2012 we’ve scoured the city of Montreal (and beyond), bringing you the best the city has to offer. As 2016 rolls to a close we’ve already begun to look at the New Year ahead we’re confident that there plenty of great new discoveries to come.

DJNL’s new look is visually stimulating while remaining clean and concise. Because looks aren’t everything, we’ve rebuilt the website from the ground up making your favourite restaurants, hotels and bars easier to find. We added several exciting new features which helps deliver even more relevant content to you. Here’s what you’ll see now.

Search

With over 500 articles covering everything from food to fashion, it can be a daunting task finding something specific on DJNL but that’s all changed now with Search. Simply click on the search icon (found on the top right corner of your screen) from your laptop or your mobile device and type what you’re looking for. Planning a party or you simply need a great pizza spot on the plateau? With search, we have you covered.

Mobile

Most Internet traffic now originates from tablets and smartphones (if you’re reading this on your smartphone, you already know now good it looks!). Knowing this it was important that we develop a DJNL that looks great and is easy to use on any device. A more mobile DJNL means finding your next favourite hotspot that much easier from anywhere. The new mobile site allows you to search or chose from one of our topics in the menu. Finding your way while you’re on your way has never been easier.

Share

Read something you like on DJNL and want to share it with your friends? Each of our articles now features social media buttons that allow you easily share what you just read with your social media circles. Simply click the Facebook, Twitter, Pinterest or Tumblr buttons found directly in the article and share instantly.

Ratings

Over the last year we have been developing a new rating system for our restaurant, hotel and bar reviews. Now you can see how we rated a restaurant before reading about it. Be sure to check out all our coming reviews to see where restaurants stand in the city. This is a real 10/10 for us!

Subscribe

Receive the latest news and updates from DJNL by subscribing to our mailing list. Don’t worry, you won’t receive useless emails that you will delete before reading. Our monthly newsletter will inform you on what we’ve covered in the last month, in case you missed something and it will provide a sneak peek on what’s coming soon.

Have a look around and enjoy your new DJNL. There’ll be lots to see and read in the coming weeks. An immense thank you to Adam Simms for the redesign and upgrade. You are a true professional.
