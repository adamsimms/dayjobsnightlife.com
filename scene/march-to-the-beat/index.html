MARCH TO THE BEAT!

February 27, 2013
Blair Dohey
The Social Scene

Spring is finally upon us and that axis can’t shift quickly enough to start heating us up!! As the northern hemisphere starts thawing out, Montreal’s music scene is also heating up with a gaggle of shows throughout the city you might want to check out! We have collected a few that might interest you!

 

 

Toronto born, Serena Ryder will be singing her heart out at The Corona Theatre on March 7th. www.theatrecoronavirginmobile.com

Adventure Club will be at New City Gas on March 9th. They are a pretty electrically eclectic bunch which happens to fit right in with this crazy venue. www.newcitygas.com

On March 12th, Pink stops by the Bell Centre for what should certainly be an unforgettable show. Anyone who has seen the AMAs this year, or any of Pink’s past performances, knows to expect the unexpected. www.centrebell.ca

Fans of UK singer/songwriter Kate Nash can check her out on March 14th at Cabaret Mile-End. Expect songs from her new album, Girl Talk. www.lemileend.org

Rihanna will ring in St. Patrick’s Day when she rocks the Bell Centre on March 17th. Will she take up an Irish accent for the day? You never know with this one. www.centrebell.ca

Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds are performing at the Metropolis on March 22nd. www.metropolismontreal.ca

Canadian rapper (and fellow east coaster) Classified will be booming through Metropolis on March 23rd. He will be serving up some Inner Ninja realness. www.metropolismontreal.ca
