OVER THE TOP

December 23, 2015
Blair Dohey
The Social Scene

Think of the biggest, craziest, wildest party you’ve ever seen. Now, think of that party kicking off on a Sunday afternoon at around 2:00pm and multiply that by a thousand percent. You may start understanding what went down at Soubois last weekend as Matinee celebrated 5 years of throwing the wildest whirlwind brunch parties that you have to see to believe. 

Soubois was packed to the rafters with snappily-dressed party goers who were not quite ready to say good-bye to their weekend. Needless to say this is not your mama’s brunch as bottle service, dancers and wild DJs keep the party moving like Saturday night never ended. In less than a year, Soubois has become the place to see and bee seen with great food, exotic ambiance and a killer party scene which made it perfect for Matinee’s 5 year anniversary party.

Adamo Mariani, Mykl Zadourian, Saman Rahbar are the men behind the magic of Matinee and for the last five years they have been perfecting their formula while building a winning brand that continues to expand its reach around the globe. They have developed a strong following around the world with people travelling far and wide to attend their brunch parties. You can’t buy loyalty, you earn it and these guys have well earned their way.

Matinee is responsible for successful parties such as the famous Yelloweek extravaganzas at Buonanotte, the heatwave at Santos and they even hit this road this year, bringing their party perfection to TIFF. They boys don’t plan on settling for the best brunch parties in Montreal. They have big plans to spread out across Canada and eventually take on the lucrative global market. Matinee is another great reason why Montreal is the premiere place to party in Canada.

Become a fan of theirs on Facebook to keep up with all the latest. You don’t want to miss out on a Matinee Brunch Party.
