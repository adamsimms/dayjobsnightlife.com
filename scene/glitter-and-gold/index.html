Glitter and Gold

November 8, 2015
Blair Dohey
The Social Scene

Last Thursday, everything that glitters was gold at Holt Renfrew at the official launch of their Holiday merchandising program. The store invited shoppers to take advantage of extended hours to get a head start on their Christmas shopping while sipping on warm cider and hot chocolate. 

10% of all sales during the evening went to support the efforts of the Children’s Wish Foundation. For those who crave a little Holts over the Holiday’s, now’s the time to go pick up those special items from Fachon and Mariage frères to Dior and Diptyque.

For more information on Holt Renfrew, check out their website and do the same for the Children’s Wish Foundation.
