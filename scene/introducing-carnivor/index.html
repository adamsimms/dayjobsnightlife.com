Introducing Carnivor

October 14, 2013
Blair Dohey
The Social Scene

Last Monday night, St. Henri was at its most dapper as Notorious Barbershop opened its doors to some very stylish men and women and welcomed Carnivor Wines to Montreal.

The evening got off to a damp start but soon the skies parted over Montreal and a bright rainbow arched across the city and over the crowd as we trickled in. As the sun went down we all gathered together to get a taste of the new cabernet sauvignon that graces the shelves of our favorite SAQ.

The cabernet sauvignon is perfect for the wine lover who looks for a packed punch. Its robust flavor lends to a perfect pairing with a delicious, juicy steak or could easily be enjoyed on its own at a 5à7 or before heading out to dinner.

Stylish guests and colourful locals blended and chatted as we sipped on the full bodied wine and ate delicious hor d’oeuvres while the popular salon offered fresh shaves, head massages and special treatments that Notorious provides to their clients. Have a look at their Facebook page here!

Great food, good wine and some of Montreal’s coolest people; I can’t possibly think of a warmer welcome for Carnivor.
