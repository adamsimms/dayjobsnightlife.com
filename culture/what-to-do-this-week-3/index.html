WHAT TO DO THIS WEEK

August 9, 2015
Blair Dohey
Culture, Pop

All Week Long:

Tennis fanatics are already enjoying the intense stress that comes with the Rogers Cup. From August 7th to the 16, Montreal Uniprix stadium will play host to this year’s men’s competitions. The brightest stars of men’s tennis including Novak Djokovic, Andy Murray, Stan Wawrinka, Milos Raonic and more are all in town competing for the top spot. For information and how to buy tickets visit couperogers.com

Montreal’s Pride festival kicks off today with a weeks worth of activities planned starting at 9:00am each day and running into the latest hours of the night. From Drag Queen shenanegins to serious discussions on topics facing the gay community to dancing, art, music and more, this week montreal will blast its true rainbow colours for all the enjoy. Visit fiertemontrealpride.com for more.

Cavalia’s hit show, Odysseo has been selling out successfully since 2011. This week the show will come to an end with the final show on Sunday, August 16th. This is the last time you have the chance to see the these beautiful animals in action. cavalia.net has all the information you will need.

Thursday:

Cheryl Besner, host of the very succesful radio show, Solo in the City will be broadcasting live from Decca 77 this Thursday night with a slew of special guests including round table guests Scarlett James and Vincent White and co-host, Dr. Laurie Betito. Breakfast Television’s Wilder Weir will also drop by. The group will be discussing the finess of flirting while guests enjoy a “discovery dinner menu”. Places are limited and the price for a three course meal will set you back $32.00, not to bad for a great dinner and what will certainly be an lively show. Reserve your place at decca77.com and be sure to let them know you will be a guest of the show and recieve a complimentary glass of white wine.

Diner en blanc – A grand tradition of mystery, dinner and dancing, the 2015 Diner en blanc will happen this Thursday at a yet to be disclosed location. The rules for this dinner are pretty tight so if you are a guest or would like to be, its important to read up and know what your getting yourself into. Wear white, bring your own table, chairs, food, plates, linens and cutlery and make sure all of your accessories are white as well. Get all the info here. Come dressed to impress and you may walk away with a prize.

Friday:

On Friday DJs from around the globe will decend upon Montreal along with thier superfans for the annual Îlesoniq EDM festival. Featuring Keisza, Deadmaus, Steve Angello and many more. For details on the two days of fun in the sun, visit the IleSoniq website here. To enjoy your festival in the best VIP has to offer, get your details here.

Have a great week!
