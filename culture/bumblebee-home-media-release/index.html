Bumblebee Home Media Release

March 31, 2019
Blair Dohey
Culture

Bumblebee was by far my favourite film in the decade spanning series of Transformers films. When the first of these movies was released back in 2007 I had such high hopes for finally seeing Optimus, Megatron and all the Transformers on the big screen. With each passing film, those hopes were diminished and just about burnt out until last year when Bumblebee changed all that.

The film was a hit and now that its finally coming to DVD next week, I wanted to bring it to your attention and show you that if I haven’t seen it yet and you’re a fan of Transformers, you don’t want to miss, even if it’s just for the crazy open sequence that blew us all away!

Bumblebee is available for download here and the DVD release is this Tuesday, April 2nd.
