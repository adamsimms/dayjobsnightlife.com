Osheaga 2015

May 24, 2015
Blair Dohey
Culture, Pop

In less than 3 months hoards of music fans from around the world will decend upon Montreal for Osheaga, Canada’s largest music and art festival. Celebrating a decade of memories and unforgettable musical moments, this year’s line-up is huge and the festival is going to be bigger than ever. Here’s a peek at some of the perfomers set to hit the stage this summer.

Headlining this years show are Florence and the Machine, Kendrick Lamar, the Black Keys, Weezer, Edward Sharpe and the Magnetic Zeros, Of Monsters and Men, Patrick Watson, The Decemeberists, Banks (pictured above), Chet Faker (pictured above), Tyler, The Creator, Angus and Julia Stone (pictured below), Art Department (pictured below), Young the Giant, Hot Chip (pictured above), Arkells, Charlie XCX (pictured below) and so many more, I can’t believe we can squeeze all this into 3 days.

Combining music and art, Osheaga is the ultimate celebration of artistic creativity in one amazing location. Grown to become one of the most hotly anticipated music events of the year, Osheaga is Canada’s answer to SXSW and Cochella in the US.

Osheaga runs from July 31st to August 2nd in Parc Jean Drapeau. Find everything you need (including the complete lineup) at osheaga.com and be sure to drop us a line if you need help finding a great restaurant, bar or hotel.

See you there!
