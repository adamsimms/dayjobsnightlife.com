SIDIM Design Show

April 23, 2016
Blair Dohey
Pop, Trend

The SIDIM Design Show is Canada’s largest gathering of design professionals and enthusiasts and one of North America’s most elegant and advanced design shows. Details of this year’s design show were unveiled at an exclusive media brunch held at Griffintown’s fashion forward restaurant, Le Richmond and featured this year’s spokesperson, Mitsou Gélinas. 

Running from Thursday, May 5th to Saturday, May 7th, the design show attracts thousands of people every year, curious about the latest furnishing and lifestyle trends, new and innovative products and much more. Spread out over Place Bonaventure’s sprawling convention space, this years show promises more decorative accessories, lighting, green products and more.

DJNL is offering two tickets to our lucky DJNL readers. All you have to do is follow this link and like our Facebook Page. Our current Facebook fans are already entered. We will be drawing names on May 1st so act quickly if you want to swim in a sea of design for free! Mitsou Gélinas will be on hand discussing her love for design.

Thursday and Friday’s exposition is reserved for design professionals and business people and Saturday is open to the public. The show features a list of activities including seminars, meetings and even speed dating. To become an exhibitor or to learn more about the show and how to get there, visit their website here.

DETAILS:

Running from May 5-7, 2016

May 5th and 6th reserved for design professionals and business people; May 7th, open to the public.
