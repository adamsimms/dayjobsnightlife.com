Walk The Line

March 19, 2016
Blair Dohey
Pop, Trend

Holt Renfrew is Canada’s cutting edge leader in high fashion for men and women. Their stores cater to those who push the style envelope beyond the confines of their comfort zones into a place that dazzles the imagination. In Montreal, Holts menswear lines are unrivalled when it comes to quality, workmanship and tailoring. Featuring a multifaceted selection of highly coveted brands like Dior, Gucci, Tom Ford and more, Holts makes it easy for us to look our best. No look is complete, however, without the right pair of shoes. 

Montreal’s Holts store recently revamped the men’s shoe department with an infusion of the hottest new brands and a sleek new look. Heading into the spring season we are going to shed those bulky winter boots for a pair of detail-oriented sneakers from Buscemi or a snug new slip-on from Marcelo Burlon. Both these new brands offer a street-inspired jolt of next-level fashion that could come only from creative designers of Italy. The Buscemi collection pops with fringe detailing, golden locks and accents, trend setting colours and only the best handworked leather. Marcelo Burlon features an even edgier collection of shoes that run more deeply with their street inspirations. These shoes for the guy with an unrelenting attitude, the rock star in us all who isn’t afraid of a little controversy.

Many of the brands featured at the Holt Renfrew men’s shoe department walk the line between street inspiration and red carpet flair. You can chose a supple pair of dress shoes from Gucci or Prada or you can chose to shake things up with a pair of their ultra cool sneakers instead. Like all rules, style rules are now made to be broken. Infusing your style with the right pair of statement shoes is a surefire way to get people talking. Offering a well rounded customer experience with choice shoe collections makes Holt Renfrew the perfect fashion destination for both your day jobs and the nightlife.

For more on Holts brands visit their website or drop by the store on Sherbrooke to see them for yourself.
