See and be Seen

March 15, 2015
Blair Dohey
Pop, Trend

Despite the cold, Montreal’s most fashionable media and other guests descended upon the Galerie Antoine Ertaskirzan for the preview launch of Bon Look’s first men’s eyewear collection.

Founded in Montreal in 2011, Bon Look provides a fashionable yet accessible alternative to higher priced prescription brands. With their remarkable designs, they also offer something that’s uniquely Montreal and we all love that.

Their new men’s line, named Tastemakers, features a collection of uniquely modern frames that are inspired by some of Montreal’s most influential tastemakers. These people have a unique vision and style all their own. Featuring names like Brandon Proust of the Montreal Canadiens, Frank and Oak co-founder and CEO, Ethan Song, Radio Radio member, Gabriel Malenfant and many more, very cool people.

The launch gathered a gaggle of people to get an up close and personal first look at the new line while they sipped wine and cocktails and chased the hunger away with crudités, breads, cheese and more, all in one of Montreal’s most one-of-a-kind art galleries.

Bon Look’s approach to accessible and fashionable prescription eyewear gives great access and flexibility to a very visible accessory.

For more information visit their website.
