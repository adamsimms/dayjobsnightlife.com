A Tropical Event Heats Up Montreal This Winter

November 18, 2018
Blair Dohey
Trend

Brighten up your life this winter – Come check out Salt & Papaya, a unique Tropical Holiday shopping experience happening one day only in December.

Salt & Papaya adds some Caribbean heat to your holiday shopping this year, featuring clothing, art and accessories all designed and made in the Caribbean. Featuring a number of designer fashions imported expressly for this one-day-only event, it’s a great moment to drop that dark winter coat and soak up the warmth invoked by these colourful and sexy fashions.

While you may still may not have a choice but wrap yourself in a winter coat, it’s what you wear underneath that brings the heat. No one ever said that winter had to be dull.

You can learn more about the designers featured at Salt & Papaya here and let them know you’re coming by confirming your presence here.

WHERE: Centre Never Apart – 7049 St Urbain St, Montreal, QC H2S 3H4

WHEN: December 8th, 2018 (From 12:00 to 5:00pm)
