JoshuaDAVID

October 1, 2014
Blair Dohey
Pop, Trend

It was a fashionable night out a couple of weeks ago as JoshuaDAVID celebrated their 5 years of success with a blowout party at their Sherbrooke West store. Having carved out a niche for themselves with top-notch service and brands in their beautiful store, the lavish party was a salute to their past and a glass raised to a prosperous future. Open since September of 2009, JoshuaDAVID has built their success on a promise to keep their clients looking and feeling their best.  Their personalized service ensures each client understands their value and their place in making the store and the people behind it so sought after. The decadent night kicked off with cocktails celebrating each of the two owners. Developed by mixologist extraordinaire, Nectar and Mixology, and featuring smooth Botanist gin, the fashionable guests enjoyed either the Joshua (Botanist Gin, Yerba Mate Tonic, Watercress) or the David (Botanist Gin, Cointreau, matcha tea, lemongrass syrup, fresh lime, egg white) or both! As the celebration continued the bubbly was popped and we toasted to the continued success of these enthusiastic entrepreneurs. A special presentation of a new dress, designed in collaboration with JoshuaDAVID by local designer, Travis Taddeo, exclusively for the event, was the talk of the night. Both Joshua and David were overwhelmed by the support they have in the city and were happy to share the night with their exclusive clientele, family and friends.
