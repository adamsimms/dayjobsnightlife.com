The Holidays At Le Richmond

December 18, 2016
Blair Dohey
Montreal, Restaurants

There are a number of reasons why I keep going back to Le Richmond. The larger-than-life decor is still as impressive as the first time I saw it, the ambiance is as lively as ever and the food keeps getting better. The main reason why I love going back to the restaurant and the market is because of the warm welcome we always receive. That’s why it is one of the best places in the city to celebrate the holidays between family and friends. From their Christmas brunch to their epic New Year’s Eve celebrations, they’re pulling out all the stops and so that we can celebrate in style.

The Holiday Brunch

Weekends during the Holiday’s at Le Richmond will be a little more festive thanks to an enchanted forest that’s taken over the terrasse and their special Christmas inspired brunch menu that features delicious soups, wonderful salmon Benedict and an amazing turkey dinner inspired dish that may be the best thing I’ve ever eaten at the restaurant.

Seeing turkey dinner listed on any menu is an instant no brainer for me and at Le Richmond I was curious to see how it would be presented. I started with a deliciously creamy carrot soup that was topped with a sugary rosemary crumble. At the same time a plate of mini sun-dried tomatoes, grilled artichoke and fresh yogurt with berries landed on the table to compliment the starter. One thing is for sure, you don’t walk away hungry from a meal at Le Richmond.

The turkey dinner was a stylish serving of turkey and blood pudding wrapped together with apples and hazelnuts and accompanied by a squash puree, green beans and of course, a savoury turkey jus with cranberries. It doesn’t get more festive than that. Ok, maybe one or two Christmas cocktails or a mimosa will make things a little more merry and bright.

We also tried the salmon Benedict which was served on a warm biscuit and topped with poached eggs, beet ricotta and a light hollandaise sauce. This dish is a one of Le Richmond’s signature brunch dishes. The table also received a wreath shaped beet salad that was fresh and popped with flavours of dill, mint and earthy beets.

We capped our meal off with a very special visit from a very special guest.

New Year’s Eve

Over the last couple of years Le Richmond has become well known for their lavish parties and this year’s New Year’s Eve gala will be no different. Promising a night of glittery glam, the theme is Midnight Blue, the menu will feature a buffet of gastronomical proportions and you should come, decked out in your evening wear finest to count down to the beginning of a brand new year!

The Market

Le Marche Italian has a slew of new decor items, gifts (including an amazing collection of Frasier Fir candles and more) and all the food and wine items necessary to bring your holidays to life. Le Marché Italien is one of my favourite places to shop in the city.
