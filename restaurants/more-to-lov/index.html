More To LOV

July 18, 2017
Blair Dohey
Restaurants

After revolutionizing vegan and vegetarian cuisine less than a year ago, LOV (local, organic and vegetarian) has already expanded to a swanky new location smack dab in the middle of downtown Montreal.

Ever since the original LOV opened earlier this year in Old Montreal, its been packed to the rafters with people who’ve raved about their authentic and inventive take on vegan cuisine. Even we were converted!

The beautiful new location takes  few cues from the original but is in itself an entirely new look with a sprawling terrasse, a private room that can be reserved for functions, a great bar space and more.

While a few menu items are expected vary, the food remains largely the same which will satisfy vegan food lovers across the city. LOV continues to defy our perceptions of what vegan food is and how it’s presented. The atmosphere challenges any of our city’s nightlife hotspots and invites Montrealer’s and visitors to our city to come in, pull up a chair and be wowed from start to finish.
