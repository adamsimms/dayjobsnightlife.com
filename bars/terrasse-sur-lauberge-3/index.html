Terrasse Sur L’Auberge

July 2, 2016
Blair Dohey
Bars, Montreal

The opening of the Terrasse sur l’Auberge has become a bit of a tradition here at DJNL. For the last 4 years we have made our way up to the beautiful rooftop terrasse of the Auberge du Vieux-Port for the annual opening party. 

The elegant terrasse is beautifully designed and serves up a wonderful selection of delicious cocktails, bubbly champagne, draft beer and more. They also offered a freshen menu of canapés and quick bites to enjoy while sipping on your favourite 5a7 beverage.

Terrasse sur l’Auberge is a great place to gather to enjoy a sun-drenched weekend cocktail, a summer 5a7 and its also one of the best places in the city to take in the annual fireworks festival (beginning Saturday, July 2). With an excellent view of the Jacques-Cartier bridge, you’ll see each vivid explosion light up the night sky without any obstruction.
