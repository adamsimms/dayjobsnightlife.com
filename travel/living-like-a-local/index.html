Living Like A Local

January 3, 2015
Blair Dohey
Blogs, Travel

If you’re like me, a fan of both visiting France and cooking French food, then you’re going to love The Farm to Table French Phrasebook. It has everything you need to vivre a la française while visiting France or cooking up a storm from the comfort of your own kitchen.

Filled with everything from handy phrases, cultural tips and words that will help you navigate weekend pop-up food markets, supermarkets, corner stores and everything in between, this compact guide will be the only thing you need when travelling and living like a local.The book also helps with understanding the multi-tiered and easily confounded French etiquette and will add to your own take on French savoir-faire. With simple translations of everything from cooking methods to every type of food imaginable, this little book is a great resource for novice to seasoned travellers and food lovers alike.

Written by French native, Victoria Mas and beautifully illustrated by Meera Lee Patel, the phrasebook is also a handy addition to your personalized culinary collection. Pick up your copy here!
